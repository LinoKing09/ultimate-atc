# Airports

Every airport is built after the [airport recipe](../airport-data.md#adding-an-airport---the-recipe): OpenStreetMap layout, facts from the national AIP (DFS; in the US the FAA Chart Supplement), procedures after VATSIM, checked against the charts.

| Airport | FIR | Runways in use | Positions | What makes it special |
| ------- | --- | -------------- | --------- | --------------------- |
| [EDDS Stuttgart](EDDS.md) | Langen | 07/25 | Delivery, Ground, Tower | The home airport: tutorials, every scenario option, de-icing pads, holding bays |
| [EDDK Cologne/Bonn](EDDK.md) | Langen | 13L/31R (06/24 and 13R/31L drawn and crossed) | Delivery, Ground, Tower | Cargo hub (UPS, FedEx, DHL), parallel guide lines, crossing of 06/24 |
| [EDDN Nuremberg](EDDN.md) | München | 10/28 | Ground, Tower | Single runway, GA and cargo aprons |
| [EDDP Leipzig/Halle](EDDP.md) | München | 08L/26R and 08R/26L **at the same time** | Delivery, Ground, Tower | DHL hub, dependent parallel approaches, runway per apron |
| [EDDW Bremen](EDDW.md) | Bremen | 08/26 | Ground (with clearances), Tower | Night rules, Beluga take-off runways drawn |
| [EDJA Memmingen](EDJA.md) | München | 06/24 | Ground, Tower | Departures and arrivals **backtrack** on the runway |
| [EDXW Sylt](EDXW.md) | Bremen | 14/32 (06/24 crossed) | Tower only | Small island airport, crossing runways |
| [KSAN San Diego](KSAN.md) | Los Angeles (KZLA) | 9/27 | Clearance Delivery, Ground, Tower | **FAA phraseology**, US weather and ATIS, busy single runway with displaced thresholds |
